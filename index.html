The values we practise

Character grows through everyday choices.

Six values guide how our students learn, collaborate and contribute.

Integrity

Choosing what is right, even when no one is watching.

Perseverance

Continuing with courage, patience and purpose.

Respect

Listening thoughtfully and valuing every person.

Empathy

Understanding others and responding with kindness.

Responsibility

Owning our actions and contributing to our community.

Care

Creating a safe, supportive place for every learner.
